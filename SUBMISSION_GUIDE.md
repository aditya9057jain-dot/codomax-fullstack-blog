## Quality checklist
- Do not commit `.env`.
- Use a strong JWT secret.
- Test register/login with a throwaway test account first.
- Create at least 2 blogs, then edit and delete one.
- Verify a user cannot update/delete another user's blog.
- Test the deployed link in an incognito window.
